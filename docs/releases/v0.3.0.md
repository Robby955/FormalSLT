# FormalSLT v0.3.0

Two predictors have been scored on the same stream. At each reporting time,
you select the one with the smaller observed loss. How large could its
average conditional loss have been over that prefix?

FormalSLT answers this question with a bound that holds on one event across
all positive reporting times. The example below uses a uniform prior over
the two predeclared predictors. Selecting one after seeing the scores incurs
a model-selection term of `log 2`. The public Brier replay uses the same
half-tilt trajectory theorem with a fixed confidence level and recorded
predictions.

## Use the theorem from another Lean package

The [downstream example](../../tests/downstream/Downstream/SelectedPredictor.lean)
imports `FormalSLT.StochasticDynamics` and proves
`Downstream.SelectedPredictor.selectedRiskBound`. It compares the two
empirical mean losses and specializes the general result to the chosen
predictor. The bound uses its observed loss, an observable quadratic-variation
term, the selection cost, and the confidence level.

Run the example from a checkout of this version with Lean 4.32.2:

```sh
make downstream
```

This builds a separate Lake package against the checkout. The theorem assumes
bounded scores in `[0, 1]`, a finite state space, a specified initial state,
and a sequence of transition kernels that may depend on the observed history.
Its risk is the average of the selected predictor’s one-step conditional
losses along the prefix. A guarantee about future predictions or stationary
risk requires additional assumptions.

## Replay the occupancy Brier bound

From the repository root:

```sh
lake exe cache get
make replay-uci357-brier
```

The target reads the tracked protocol, prediction CSV, and certificate.
The verifier recomputes the summaries for the 8,224 monitored observations
and invokes Lean on the certificate arithmetic. The all-sensor logistic
predictor has lower observed Brier loss (about 0.061198) than the
training-prevalence baseline (about 0.165322). The checked upper bound on
the selected predictor’s conditional prefix risk at 95% confidence is
**18317/250000 = 0.073268**.

The forecast reconstruction is retrospective. The statistical interpretation
requires both candidates’ forecasts to be available before each corresponding
outcome. The archived occupancy data do not establish historical label timing.
The [replay instructions](../../applications/brier_monitor/README.md#replay-the-occupancy-certificate)
give the expected verifier output and distinguish replaying the shipped
predictions from refitting the models.

## Other additions since v0.2.0

- Predictable-strategy PAC-Bayes bounds allow model and strategy posteriors
  to be chosen after observation and account for each choice separately.
  [Model and strategy theorem](../../FormalSLT/PACBayes/ForwardPredictableStrategyPACBayes.lean)
- A computable countable betting mixture evaluates the active experts and a
  closed-form tail at each step.
  [Sleeping-expert construction](../../FormalSLT/AnytimeValid/CountableSleepingPredictableBettingMixture.lean)
- The Brier monitor also supports replay of a Good Judgment Project forecasting
  example and verification of chronological tabular prediction streams.
  [Application and CLI](../../applications/brier_monitor/README.md)

Lean and Mathlib remain pinned to 4.32.2. The four supported topic imports
and the 19 declarations in the v0.2 signature snapshot carry forward.
The [API policy](../api-stability.md) describes the scope of patch-level
compatibility. Use the [citation metadata](../../CITATION.cff) when citing
this version and the [release checklist](../public-release-checklist.md) for
